## Description (4000 characters max)

**Vamoose: Calm in Chaos**

When disaster strikes, you don't rise to the occasion—you fall to your level of preparation. Vamoose helps your family get ready before emergencies happen.

**SMART GO-BAG CHECKLISTS**
Build the perfect emergency kit with intelligent checklists customized for your region. Whether you're preparing for hurricanes on the Gulf Coast, earthquakes in California, wildfires in the West, or winter storms in the Northeast, Vamoose recommends exactly what you need.

• Regional presets for 10 disaster zones across the US
• Track what you have and what you still need
• Category-organized items: water, food, first aid, documents, and more
• Progress tracking shows your overall preparedness score

**HOUSEHOLD MANAGEMENT**
Keep your family connected and organized during emergencies.

• Add family members with contact information
• Set designated meetup locations if you get separated
• Track everyone's safety status in one place

**REAL-TIME WEATHER ALERTS**
Stay informed with official alerts from the National Weather Service.

• Emergency alerts highlighted prominently
• Detailed information including affected areas and instructions
• Filter alerts by your state
• Pull-to-refresh for latest updates

**EMERGENCY RESOURCES**
Quick access to critical phone numbers and official resources—no searching required.

• 911 Emergency Services
• FEMA Disaster Assistance
• American Red Cross
• 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline
• Poison Control Center
• Direct links to Ready.gov and official preparedness guides

**BUILT FOR EMERGENCIES**
Vamoose is designed to work when you need it most.

• Offline-first: Your data is stored locally on your device
• Fast and lightweight
• Clean, distraction-free interface
• No account required to get started

**YOUR PRIVACY MATTERS**
We believe your emergency preparedness data is deeply personal. Vamoose stores your information locally on your device. We don't sell your data, and we never will.
